Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Incentiv Assistant may handle information when you use its community management and points distribution features on Discord.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

On this page

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Incentiv Assistant, a Discord bot used for community management and points distribution in servers where it has been invited and configured.

Discord also processes information under its own privacy policy. This policy describes the bot operator's practices and is not a substitute for Discord's policies.

2. Information We Handle

To provide the bot's features, Incentiv Assistant may handle the following categories of information:

  • Discord User IDs: to identify users interacting with the bot.
  • Discord Server IDs: to associate activity and configuration with a server.
  • Usernames: to identify or display users within bot features.
  • Bot activity and commands: information about interactions with bot commands, where processed or recorded.
  • Points or balances: values used by the bot's points distribution features.

The exact fields recorded, whether command contents or logs are retained, and whether additional technical data is collected must be confirmed against the bot's actual implementation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide community management and points distribution features.
  • Associate users and points records with the relevant Discord server.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported errors or abuse.
  • Maintain the operation, integrity, and security of the bot.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes unless permitted by applicable law or otherwise explained to users.

4. Storage and Retention

The operator reports that bot data is stored in a database hosted on the operator's own VPS environment.

Data is intended to be retained for as long as necessary to operate the bot and provide its features. Actual retention periods, backup practices, and deletion procedures depend on the system configuration and must be confirmed by the operator.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

The operator reports that user information is not intentionally shared with other parties.

However, the VPS hosting provider may technically process or access data as part of hosting, maintenance, security, or troubleshooting. The operator should confirm the provider's access and data-processing arrangements before publishing this statement as a complete description of data sharing.

Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

6. Security

The operator should use reasonable safeguards to protect stored information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Specific safeguards, such as access controls, encryption, backups, and monitoring, have not been confirmed in this policy.

7. Your Choices and Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your use of Incentiv Assistant, you can contact the operator using the details below.

Requests to access, correct, or delete information will be reviewed where reasonably possible and subject to technical limitations, applicable law, and any necessary retention requirements. A specific deletion process has not yet been confirmed.

8. Children and Age Requirements

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local laws. This policy does not establish a separate age threshold for Incentiv Assistant.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the bot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date where appropriate.
